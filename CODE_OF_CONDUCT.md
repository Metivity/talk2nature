# Community conduct

Everyone should be able to ask questions, disagree with a claim and contribute useful work without harassment. This policy covers Talk2Nature's issues, pull requests and project-managed discussions.

Be respectful and specific. Critique evidence and code, not people. Welcome newcomers, differing backgrounds and corrections. Credit others' work. Do not post discriminatory abuse, threats, sexual harassment, repeated unwanted contact, private personal information or knowingly misleading research claims. Respect animal welfare and sensitive ecological locations.

Report concerns privately to Raviv at raviv@metivity.com with “Talk2Nature conduct” in the subject. Share only the minimum context needed; do not post the report in a public issue. Raviv is the current sole maintainer and report recipient, so an independent appeals panel is not yet available. If your concern involves the maintainer, GitHub's own reporting mechanisms remain available for conduct on its platform.

Responses may include asking for a correction, removing content, restricting participation or a temporary/permanent ban from project-managed spaces, proportionate to severity and recurrence. An explanation and reconsideration route should be provided when safe. Do not retaliate against good-faith reports. Report details should be shared only as needed for handling; absolute confidentiality or a response deadline is not promised.

This is an original, minimal project policy, informed by [GitHub's community guidance](https://opensource.guide/building-community/).

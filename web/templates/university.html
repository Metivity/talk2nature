WHY ASK A UNIVERSITY?

where the seams are.

READY FOR REVIEW

Invented audio.
Real workflow feedback.

  • Local browser workflow and synthetic test sound
  • Timed labels, original notes and conversion report
  • Exact revision and software version can be recorded
  • One small task; negative results are useful

NOT READY FOR A FIELD STUDY

First, earn the
scientific gates.

  • No Talk2Nature animal-communication model
  • No accepted research recordings or public data intake
  • Ultrasonic and synchronized audio/video workflows are unverified
  • No independent scientific reviewer or institutional protocol approval

A USEFUL FIRST CONTRIBUTION

Show us one thing
the tool gets wrong.

Try the example, then send a short, redacted report: what you tried, the device and software versions, what you expected, what happened and whether the workflow solves a real task. Do not send recordings or private research data.

A SEPARATE SCIENCE REVIEW

Check one claim
against the evidence.

The browser exercise tests software; it does not validate the biology. For a focused literature review, we invite critical readers to check our source-linked note on Japanese great-tit call sequences and receiver responses.

THE BOUNDED QUESTION

What do the cited receiver-response experiments support about call sequence and order, and which competing explanations remain unresolved?
  • Check one statement against the cited paper and exact section, table or figure.
  • Flag an alternative explanation, missing control or limit on generalization.
  • Name the smallest public-evidence check that could change your assessment.

This is a literature critique, not a request to plan or conduct playback, share recordings or unpublished data, endorse the project, or join a partnership. Reports are public; do not include personal details or sensitive locations.

READY FOR REVIEW

Invented audio.
Real workflow feedback.

  • Local browser workflow and synthetic test sound
  • Timed labels, original notes and conversion report
  • Exact revision and software version can be recorded
  • One small task; negative results are useful

NOT READY FOR A FIELD STUDY

First, earn the
scientific gates.

  • No Talk2Nature animal-communication model
  • No accepted research recordings or public data intake
  • Ultrasonic and synchronized audio/video workflows are unverified
  • No independent scientific reviewer or institutional protocol approval

A USEFUL FIRST CONTRIBUTION

Show us one thing
the tool gets wrong.

Try the example, then send a short, redacted report: what you tried, the device and software versions, what you expected, what happened and whether the workflow solves a real task. Do not send recordings or private research data.

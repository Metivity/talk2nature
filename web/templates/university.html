THE BOUNDED QUESTION

What do the cited receiver-response experiments support about call sequence and order, and which competing explanations remain unresolved?
  • Check one statement against the cited paper and exact section, table or figure.
  • Flag an alternative explanation, missing control or limit on generalization.
  • Name the smallest public-evidence check that could change your assessment.

This is a literature critique, not a request to plan or conduct playback, share recordings or unpublished data, endorse the project, or join a partnership. Reports are public; do not include personal details or sensitive locations.

THE BOUNDED QUESTION · MOBILE AUDIO

Could a small no-animal reference test tell us anything useful about phone and app-path variability without being mistaken for calibration?
  • Separate microphone, operating-system/browser processing and app-path effects.
  • Decide whether reference recordings and task-specific uncertainty are useful at all.
  • Keep device metadata minimal and avoid animal, participant or location data.

This is a methods critique, not an approved protocol or request to collect wildlife audio. Physical Android/iOS behavior, including iOS Safari, remains unverified.

THE NETWORK QUESTION

Build a useful network
without collecting everything.

For birds, established tools already support sound identification and large-scale analysis, recording archives, and biodiversity media metadata. That is one mature corner of the field, not a solution for every animal or a way to establish what a signal means. Talk2Nature should connect careful observation context and provenance to existing tools, not ask every lab to upload its audio into a new central bucket. BirdNET-Analyzer · Macaulay Library audio guidance · GBIF download formats.

01 / AVAILABLE NOW

Review the workflow

Try synthetic sessions, local capture and file tools. Recordings stay with the user; there is no Talk2Nature upload or shared recording server.

02 / NEXT, IF A LAB ASKS

Design one study path

First agree on one research question, device path, labels, rights, retention and withdrawal with the institution. Keep any real study files in its approved storage.

03 / LATER, IF REPEATED

Share safe discovery metadata

If several groups need the same thing, test a federated catalog: each institution controls its files; only reviewed, permissioned records become discoverable.

That last design is a proposal, not a service. Talk2Nature's private server is a local, owner-only synthetic-metadata rehearsal. No cloud-hosted research workspace, recording intake or university network is available today.

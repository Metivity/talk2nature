This tool marks sounds and observed context. It does not identify species or translate animals. Files stay in this tab until you choose to export labels. No recordings are sent to Talk2Nature.

01 / OPEN A RECORDING

A moment to examine.

Choose a WAV up to two minutes long, or try the example. Use only recordings you have permission to inspect.

Supported files & saved sessions

WAV · mono/stereo · PCM 16/24/32-bit or float32 · up to 120 seconds and 25 MB. MP3 and M4A are not supported. For a saved Station session, unzip it first and choose one WAV. Its journal JSON is separate from this tool’s labels.

No microphone access or background recording. Nothing is saved automatically. Export your labels before closing or replacing this recording. The example contains computer-generated tones, not animal sounds.

Start with a WAV or the example. Then mark a sound and save your labels.

02 / INSPECT THE SOUND

Drag across the waveform to mark an interval. On any device, you can also enter exact start and end times below.

Playback is only for your own review. Use headphones away from animals; do not broadcast recordings to them.

Recording identifiers

Use aliases, not personal names or sensitive locations. These fields are included in exported labels. The original filename is omitted.

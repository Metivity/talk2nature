WHY KEEP THESE SEPARATE?

Research methods depend on the question.

Published work has tested annotation aggregation for specific call-detection tasks and used adjudication in a defined whale-density workflow. Those methods do not establish one universal event-matching or consensus rule. A separate audio-encoder study evaluates caller identity and call type; those tasks do not measure semantic decoding. Read the project’s source reviews before proposing a new evaluation.

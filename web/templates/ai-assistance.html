PROJECT TRANSPARENCY

AI can help us work.
It cannot verify our claims.

Generative AI and coding assistants have helped develop software, documentation, research summaries and plans. We publish what we know, what we did not record, and who remains accountable.

What we know

AI tools have been used across project development, writing and research organization. Historical model, version and per-contribution records are incomplete. This is a high-level disclosure, not a complete tool-by-tool inventory.

Where a research note uses AI assistance, it should still identify the original sources and state what was reviewed. No model-generated summary is independent scientific review. A passing software test is not evidence that a biological claim is true.

Human accountability

  • Human contributors remain responsible for the code, source interpretation, rights, privacy, security and decisions they submit.
  • AI output is a draft or aid to analysis, never an animal label, ground truth or proof of meaning.
  • Contributors should verify primary sources, check rights and dependencies, review generated code, and run relevant tests.
  • Private recordings, participant information, credentials and sensitive wildlife locations do not belong in AI prompts.

How contributors disclose use

When AI materially assists a contribution, describe what it helped with, which tool or model and version if known, and what a person checked. Do not submit prompt transcripts or confidential work logs. If the model or version was not recorded, say so; do not guess. Contributors can choose to work without generative AI.

Funding rules can be stricter

Funder requirements apply to the proposal and funded work covered by that call; they are not universal open-source rules. NLnet's policy v2.0, checked October 26, 2026, prohibits GenAI-written proposals and GenAI-produced funded deliverables and calls for disclosure of significant pre-existing AI-generated code. See the official NLnet policy and its FAQ. Talk2Nature's funding register marks the current CodeSupply call as not recommended while fit and codebase provenance are unresolved.

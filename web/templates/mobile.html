SMALL SESSIONS. CAREFUL RECORDS.

What you can do today.

  • Start a local microphone session of up to five minutes while the app stays visible.
  • Capture sound candidates and mark naturally observed behavior or voices.
  • Review and export clips and notes; explore a WAV in the sound desk.
  • Optionally save public app pages and tools for offline use.

What stays yours.

Audio and notes stay in working memory until you export. The offline cache contains only public pages, code and artwork. It never saves recordings or private workspace responses. Export before closing, replacing a session or updating the app. Downloads remain on your device.

Microphone permission is separate from installation. Record only where permitted, including permission from people who may be audible. Observe naturally; do not provoke an animal.

To remove offline app files, clear this site’s storage through your browser settings. Removing a home-screen icon alone may leave browser data.

BUILDING IN THE OPEN

Help shape the next release.

We need real device checks, independent workflow reviews and a qualified scientific collaborator. The next native-app candidate is a dedicated Android station, with visible recording controls and tested offline recovery. Automated animal playback and general translation are not available.

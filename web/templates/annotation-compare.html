A SMALL, HONEST COMPARISON

Overlap is a place to look closer.

Exact WAV checksums and recording settings must match, and the declared annotator IDs must differ. A pairwise time overlap does not establish label quality. Context is compared only when both notes name an independently observed context. No file leaves this page; nothing is saved in browser storage.

Research precedent: one blue-whale study tested two aggregation methods for two call-detection tasks against separate expert-annotated sets. That does not establish behavior or meaning, or justify a universal consensus rule. This tool keeps each observer's labels separate.

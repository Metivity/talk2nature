WHAT THIS CAN TELL YOU

Is the browser processing input?

The check shows whether processed samples arrive, how long they arrive, and whether any approach the digital ceiling. Silence can still produce samples.

A completed check does not certify the microphone. Phone processing can change sound, and a quiet animal call may still be missed.

For wildlife, start only where recording is permitted, keep a respectful distance, and never play sounds to prompt a response.

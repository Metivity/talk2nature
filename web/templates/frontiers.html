01 / ANIMALS · AUDIO + VIDEO

Hear a call.
See the setting.

Connect sound events with visible movement and social context. Existing pose and behavior tools give us a starting point. A camera can help identify a candidate animal; uncertainty and tracking errors still need review.

Our proposed test: does adding sound improve prediction beyond video alone on animals the model has never seen?

Models, limits & the next experiment →

02 / PLANTS · A DIFFERENT SENSOR WORLD

Look beyond
the audible.

Plants respond to chemical cues and emit measurable signals. Some acoustic work concerns ultrasound, outside the reliable range of ordinary phone microphones. A phone image also cannot show internal calcium activity.

Our proposed starting point: an environment journal and careful physiological measurements, designed with a plant scientist.

Chemical sensing → · Moth–plant experiments →

03 / FUNGI · SLOW ELECTRICAL PATTERNS

Change the instrument.
Change the timescale.

Electrical measurements open another line of inquiry. They require suitable electrodes and careful controls. A repeating pattern can be interesting before we know whether it carries information to a receiver.

Our proposed starting point: collaborator-led measurement validation, with environmental and instrument controls.

04 / WONDER + SCIENTIFIC CARE

Keep the question.
Test the explanation.

The water-and-ice work you may know is associated with Masaru Emoto. Published reports and a statistical critique belong in the record. They do not give us an established way to communicate with water.

Our approach: show claims alongside their limits, and let reproducible evidence guide what we build.

ONE EXPERIENCE, A CAREFUL RESEARCH PIPELINE

Simple in your hand.
Traceable behind the scenes.

  1. 1 · NoticeRecord a short session or open a sound. Add what you directly observed.
  2. 2 · KeepSave it on your device. Listen notebooks keep audio and observations together.
  3. 3 · Review · plannedChoose whether to contribute. Check privacy, permissions, quality and the study protocol.
  4. 4 · Learn · plannedUse approved, versioned datasets. Publish only reviewed material with publication permission.

The first two steps work today. Shared recording intake, camera capture, automated identification and model training remain in development. A public catalog is available; a public recording database is not yet open.

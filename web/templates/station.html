    THE LARGER IDEA

    Many listening places.
    One careful learning process.

    Next device

    A dedicated phone

    A native station with visible recording status, schedules, interruption recovery and a review queue. Android is the proposed first target. Unattended capture needs actual device tests.

    Research direction

    An encounter model

    Learn recurring sounds, their timing and observed context. Compare what follows a person’s voice with matched quiet periods. Retain absence and uncertainty, not just interesting clips.

    Separate experiment

    A shared signal

    A scientist-led Signal Lab could explore a small, voluntary exchange. Generated calls and adaptive playback need controlled response evidence and welfare review before animal use.

    The browser prototype runs a simple level detector. These future features, cloud collection, TV pairing, smart-speaker integrations and trained encounter models are not implemented.

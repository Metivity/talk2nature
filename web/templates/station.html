    THE LARGER IDEA

    Many listening places.
    One careful learning process.

    Android source preview

    A dedicated phone

    An experimental native Android project has user-started foreground capture, a visible Stop control, 1-, 4- and 8-hour limits, encrypted bounded storage and manual review/export. Its notification is configured without sound, vibration or indicator lights. It is not a signed APK or field-tested release; hardware behavior remains unverified.

    Read the source and build limits ↗
    Research direction

    An encounter model

    Learn recurring sounds, their timing and observed context. Compare what follows a person’s voice with matched quiet periods. Retain absence and uncertainty, not just interesting clips.

    Separate experiment

    A shared signal

    A scientist-led Signal Lab could explore a small, voluntary exchange. Generated calls and adaptive playback need controlled response evidence and welfare review before animal use.

    The browser prototype runs a simple level detector, and the Android source is a local-only prototype. Uploads, trained encounter models, TV pairing, smart-speaker integrations and animal translation are not implemented.

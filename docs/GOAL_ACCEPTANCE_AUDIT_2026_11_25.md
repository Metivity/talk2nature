## The next decisions

1. **Physical workflow:** make a named phone available, record model/OS/browser or installed mode, and run the no-animal device protocol. Then use the separately authorized first-use protocol with a willing newcomer. A simulator or another maintainer rerun cannot substitute.
2. **Research feedback:** two targeted messages are prepared privately and remain unsent pending the founder's explicit approval. The first seeks public artifact clarification from a paper author; the second asks for a bounded evaluation-design judgment. Author clarification, independent review, endorsement and partnership are different outcomes.
3. **Discovery:** inspect the existing Google sitemap submission after processing or a meaningful state change. Preserve the recorded error until a successful fetch is observed; do not repeatedly resubmit or change working files without a diagnosis.
4. **Benchmark:** consider a verified artifact or qualified reply before changing the current deferral. Apply the existing rights, leakage, label and execution gates; a reply does not itself authorize acquisition or training.

Until these external inputs are available, keep the interface stable unless an actual defect is found. Further counts, duplicate invitations or speculative features are not substitutes for the open acceptance evidence.

# Focused methods review: Japanese-tit receiver responses

Updated October 13, 2026. This brief invites an independent critique of a bounded literature summary. It is not a study protocol, a request for animal data or playback, an endorsement request, or evidence that Talk2Nature can interpret calls.

## Review question

What do the cited receiver-response experiments support about Japanese great-tit call order and source linkage under the tested conditions? Which alternative explanations remain, and do the competing 2024 accounts make predictions that the existing experiments can distinguish?

The [research note](https://metivity.github.io/talk2nature/research/bird-call-syntax/) gives the source-linked findings, study snapshots, limits and exact review depth. The pointers below are a map into that note and the linked papers, not a substitute for checking the original source.

## Questions a reviewer could answer

Answer only the parts you can assess from sources actually inspected. “Cannot assess from the available evidence” is useful.

1. For the 2016 component and order experiments, which observed behaviors and contrasts support an order-sensitive receiver response? Does the natural/reversed comparison distinguish a meaningful combination from effects of temporal proximity, sequence familiarity or other learned conventions? Are there endpoint-specific limits to the inference?
2. For the 2017 test, do the ABC–tää / tää–ABC contrast and the ABC–zi ordering controls support an ordering rule for this particular novel sequence? What do the sample, selected stimuli and comparison with earlier-year ABC–D data allow one to generalize?
3. What does the 2022 one/two-speaker result establish about source linkage, and what does it leave open about the receiver's mechanism? Does it independently replicate the earlier biological result, extend the same research line, or both in a narrower sense?
4. Do Minimal Compositionality and Bird Implicatures make distinct predictions for any cited condition, or can both accommodate the reported source-separation result with different assumptions? What smallest check of the public papers would clarify that judgment?
5. If a future experiment is needed, what outcome and control would distinguish the accounts while remaining subject to qualified scientific and animal-welfare review? Please describe the evidential distinction, not a ready-to-run playback protocol.

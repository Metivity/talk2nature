# Focused methods review: Japanese-tit receiver responses

Updated October 14, 2026. This brief invites an independent critique of a bounded literature summary. It is not a study protocol, a request for animal data or playback, an endorsement request, or evidence that Talk2Nature can interpret calls.

## Review question

What do the cited receiver-response experiments support about Japanese great-tit call order and source linkage under the tested conditions? Which alternative explanations remain, and do the competing 2024 accounts make predictions that the existing experiments can distinguish?

The [research note](https://metivity.github.io/talk2nature/research/bird-call-syntax/) gives the source-linked findings, study snapshots, limits and exact review depth. The pointers below are a map into that note and the linked papers, not a substitute for checking the original source.

## Questions a reviewer could answer

Answer only the parts you can assess from sources actually inspected. “Cannot assess from the available evidence” is useful.

1. For the 2016 component/order experiments and 2017 novel-sequence test, which contrasts support order-sensitive receiver responses? What alternatives remain after the component, reversal, novelty and call-type controls, and what limits follow from the samples, selected stimuli and comparison with earlier-year data?
2. What does the 2022 one/two-speaker result establish about source linkage, and what does it leave open about mechanism? Does it independently replicate the earlier result, extend the same research line, or both in a narrower sense?
3. Do Minimal Compositionality and Bird Implicatures make distinct predictions for any cited condition, or can both accommodate the source-separation result under different assumptions? What smallest public-source check could clarify that judgment?
4. How should the 2025 Carolina-wren/great-tit study affect cross-species generalization? It found receiver responses to an allopatric call sequence but did not reverse call order; what does that add about receiver response, and what remains untested about order or meaning?
5. If a future experiment is needed, what outcome and control would distinguish the accounts while remaining subject to qualified scientific and animal-welfare review? Describe the evidential distinction, not a ready-to-run playback protocol.

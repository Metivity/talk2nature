# Open-source scope and reuse

Reviewed October 4, 2026. This document explains the repository's existing licenses and contribution boundaries; it does not replace their legal text or provide trademark or patent clearance.

## The contribution we are making

Talk2Nature develops accessible observation, annotation and comparison tools, a cited research library and documented exports. General animal translation remains a research ambition. Existing work by [Earth Species Project](https://earthspecies.org/what-we-do/), [Project CETI](https://www.projectceti.org/) and the [BirdNET team](https://github.com/birdnet-team/birdnet) is part of the field we learn from. These organizations are not represented as partners. Prefer a compatible export or a contribution to an existing tool over duplicating a mature research platform.

## Different artifacts, different permissions

| Artifact | Current boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Original software | Apache-2.0. Use, modification, redistribution and commercial use are allowed subject to its terms. A compliant fork may be proprietary; sharing every improvement back is not required. |
| Original public summaries in `content/` | CC BY 4.0. Reuse and adaptation, including commercial reuse, require the applicable credit, license link and change indication. |
| Third-party papers, recordings, code and weights | Their own terms apply. A citation, download or public repository is not a blanket permission to redistribute or use commercially. |
| Participant data and precise locations | No public intake is open. Future collection, training and publication each require their own review; a code license supplies no consent. |
| Project name and marks | Apache-2.0 section 6 grants no general trademark permission. Attribution must not imply affiliation or an official service. Talk2Nature remains a working name, not a claim of registration or clearance. |

The [Apache license](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0) grants rights from contributors, including a defined patent grant; it does not clear unrelated third-party patents. Retain applicable licenses, attribution and change notices when reusing code. The [CC BY deed](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/deed.en) is a readable guide to the summary license; its legal code controls.

The [Open Source Definition](https://opensource.org/osd) permits commercial uses and does not allow a field-of-use ban. Project safety rules and hosted-service admission decisions are separate from the open-source license. Do not append a “research only,” “noncommercial,” or “only ethical uses” restriction to Apache-licensed code while describing that artifact as unchanged open source.

For example, the [NatureLM-audio model card](https://huggingface.co/EarthSpeciesProject/NatureLM-audio) currently lists CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. That model is not an automatically eligible dependency for a paid service. Pin and review each actual model, code component and dataset separately before integration; no weights were added by this review.

## Contributions and sustainable services

Contributors retain their copyrights and must have the right to submit their work under the stated license. Record origin, upstream version, applicable license and changes for external material. Disclose substantive AI assistance and check its output. The current contribution process requests no copyright assignment; see [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md).

Paid hosting, workflow integration, training or support could fund maintenance while the published code remains open. These are possible services, not current offers or validated demand. Previously granted licenses cannot simply be recalled when a business model changes. Control of this repository's releases does not prevent others from maintaining compliant forks.

Open source is a licensing choice, not a nonprofit registration. The funding applicant and legal operator remain unconfirmed. Before accepting funds or data, resolve who owns and operates the relevant service, contributor rights and any organizational agreement. Before a substantial brand campaign, complete name clearance in the intended markets; before commercializing a specific invention, obtain relevant patent advice.

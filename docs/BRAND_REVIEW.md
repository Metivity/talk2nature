# Talk2Nature name: preliminary risk review

**Checked October 8, 2026.** This is a limited brand and public-record scan, not legal advice, a trademark clearance opinion, or a finding of infringement.

## Finding

There is a **meaningful brand and discoverability concern**, especially because `Talk2Nature` is naturally read aloud as “talk to nature,” while an established birdwatching-technology company, TT Nature, uses the tagline “Let’s Talk To Nature Together.” TT Nature describes bird products and AI recognition on its own [About Us page](https://ttnature.com/pages/about-us). The products are not identical to Talk2Nature’s research tools, and this evidence alone does not establish a legal conflict. It does make the overlap more relevant than a coincidental use in an unrelated category.

This review found **no exact-wordmark result** in the limited searches below for `Talk2Nature` or `Talk to Nature` in Israel or the United States. That is useful negative evidence, not clearance: similar marks, goods and services, unregistered commercial use, and other countries still need review.

## Why the name is hard to own as a brand

`Talk2Nature` is memorable and directly expresses Raviv’s original ambition. The same plain-language quality makes it less distinctive in speech and search: people may write “Talk to Nature,” “Talk To Nature,” or “Talk2Nature,” and find other nature-conversation projects or products. TT Nature’s exact tagline strengthens that concern for bird-related technology and nature audiences.

The current visual identity and the descriptor “open research tools for animal communication” help explain this project’s scientific role, but they do not resolve trademark rights or guarantee search visibility. Treat that as a communications advantage, not legal protection.

## Recommendation

Keep the name as a **working project name** for the current repository and early prototypes; there is not enough evidence to accuse anyone of infringement or to declare the name unavailable. Before a custom domain, app-store launch, major public-relations campaign, or trademark filing, commission a jurisdiction- and service-specific clearance search.

Because the project is still early, I recommend exploring a more distinctive, coined umbrella brand before those investments. “Talk to nature” can remain the mission idea or explanatory language even if the project’s public brand changes. Do not choose or file a replacement name until its own spelling, pronunciation, domains, app listings, and relevant marks have been screened.

The clearance brief should use the intended legal operator and actual target markets, then search at least the relevant software (Nice class 9), scientific/technology and hosted-software services (class 42), and education/training (class 41) descriptions, plus related goods/services counsel identifies. The USPTO says a comprehensive search includes similar marks and unregistered/common-law use; WIPO cautions that its global database does not replace relevant national or regional registers.

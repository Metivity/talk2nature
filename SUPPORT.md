# Support

Talk2Nature is an early-stage independent project maintained by Raviv Yatom. Support is best-effort; there is no response-time commitment, paid support, public recording intake, or hosted research service.

## Where to start

- **Try a research workflow:** follow the [researcher evaluation kit](docs/RESEARCHER_START.md) using its synthetic examples, or use the [Sound Handoff Lab](https://metivity.github.io/talk2nature/tools/interop/) for the Listen-to-Audacity file check.
- **Report a reproducible software problem or suggest a change:** use the relevant [GitHub issue form](https://github.com/Metivity/talk2nature/issues/new/choose). Include the public page or exact revision, device/browser, steps, expected result and actual result. A failed workflow is useful evidence.
- **Correct or question a research summary:** use the [source and question helper](https://metivity.github.io/talk2nature/contribute/source/) or the research-question issue form. Link the original source, say what you reviewed, and state the limit of the claim.
- **Report a security or privacy concern:** follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and keep it out of public issues.
- **Report a conduct concern:** follow [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) and use its private route.

Do not send or post animal recordings, participant or personal research data, credentials, private environment files, or precise sensitive wildlife locations. Current tools are not an upload route. Do not include local usernames or filesystem paths in a public reproduction report.

There is no discussion forum or mailing list at present. Public issues are the reviewable project channel; email is for the private cases described above and individual interest conversations. Please do not interpret a delayed reply as acceptance, review approval, research enrollment or a partnership. See [governance](GOVERNANCE.md) for how decisions are made and [current status](docs/STATUS.md) for known limits.
